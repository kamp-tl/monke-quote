monke quote

monke quote is a net art piece.

the objective is to type the randomly generated quote exactly the same as you see, as fast as you can.

there is a timer to track the speed that you are typing at and add a sense of urgency.

once the quote is correctly inputted, the typed quote is populated into the background and the next quote is generated.

once the wallpaper has five quotes, the textbox and the timer disappear to reveal wall of quotes that you have typed.

a unique wall is created each time monke quote is run.
